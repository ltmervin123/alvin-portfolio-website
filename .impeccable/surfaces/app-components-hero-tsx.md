# Surface Brief: Hero Section (app/components/Hero.tsx)

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- **Scope:** Hero landing viewport (`app/components/Hero.tsx`)
- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Audience:** Engineering Hiring Managers, Technical Recruiters, Founders & Product Leads.
- **Job:** Evaluate Alvincent Sangco's full-stack & AI engineering qualifications within 30-90 seconds and convert to project exploration or interview contact.

STORY: In 5 seconds, the technical reviewer grasps Alvincent's rare hybrid mastery—combining rapid, polished web application delivery with robust backend systems, Redis queues, and production AI pipelines. They immediately spot verifiable projects and download his credentials.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height viewport centered on an expansive Lora headline naming Alvincent Sangco and his title in italic terracotta. Followed by a 68ch grounded value proposition detailing backend & AI engineering capabilities, dual high-contrast action buttons with hover physics, and an anchored production track record ledger.
FORM: Typography-first editorial layout with bold headline, concise value proposition, and dual CTAs, as confirmed by the user.
