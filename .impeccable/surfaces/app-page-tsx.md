# Surface Brief: Portfolio Homepage (app/page.tsx)

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- **Scope:** Full portfolio surface (`app/page.tsx` and all child components: Header, Hero, About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Contact, Footer)
- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Audience:** Engineering Hiring Managers, Technical Recruiters, Founders & Product Leads.
- **Job:** Evaluate Alvincent Sangco's full-stack & AI engineering qualifications within 30-90 seconds and convert to project exploration, credential download, or direct interview contact.
- **Action:** Primary action to navigate to projects and contact, explore live deployments, view project screenshot galleries, and download resume dossier.
- **Proof:** Snappcheck reference check automation (100+ candidates weekly with Claude/Gemini), 40% API latency optimization via Redis and Mongoose aggregations, production Next.js 16/React 19 & Node architecture.
- **Constraints:** Full redesign adopting Neo-Mirai design language (https://impeccable.style/neo-mirai/): Chakra Petch, Zen Old Mincho, Azeret Mono; warm paper, midnight indigo, amber gold, and sun vermilion palette; Japanese vertical poetry seals, ticket-style pills, and hairline ledger lines.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Tokyo-2042 retro-futurist Japanese cyber-humanist engineering portfolio inspired by Neo-Mirai, rejecting sterile corporate portfolios and generic card grids in favor of an authentic architectural ledger, sumi ink and rice paper textures, bold geometric sun/gold accents, and vertical literary typography.
OWN-WORLD: Palette rooted in Neo-Mirai: `--paper` (#F4F0E8 warm tactile parchment) and `--night` (#14191C midnight indigo) with `--gold` (#D99B26 amber), `--sun` (#D34E24 Japanese vermilion), hairline boundary borders (`--line`), and tactile 112px atmospheric grid. Typography pairing cybernetic `Chakra Petch`, poetic literary serif `Zen Old Mincho`, and cryptographic code font `Azeret Mono`.
STORY: In 5 seconds, technical leaders perceive Alvincent Sangco as a hybrid full-stack & AI systems architect. They navigate through a Japanese retro-futuristic exhibition ledger—from an impactful asymmetric hero with vertical poem seal to an interactive high-contrast Agenda timeline, an architectural technical spec ledger, perforated ticket project showcases, and a direct dispatch terminal.
