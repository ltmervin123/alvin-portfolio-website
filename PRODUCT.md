## Product Purpose

The primary portfolio, technical showcase, and conversion surface for Alvincent Sangco. Its purpose is to demonstrate engineering depth, system design capabilities, and end-to-end shipping velocity to convert visitors into interviews, contract discussions, and collaborative opportunities.

## Positioning

A full-stack product engineer who rapidly turns ideas into polished, end-to-end web apps. Unlike generic frontend developers or theoretical engineers, Alvincent combines rapid interface execution with robust, production-tested backend systems—integrating real-world AI pipelines (RAG, media processing, LLM evaluation) and optimizing API performance for low latency and high reliability.

## Operating Context

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Architecture:** Single-page responsive Next.js application (App Router) with fast section navigation (Hero, About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Contact, Footer).
- **Stack:** Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Lucide icons, simple-icons, Vercel Analytics.
- **Lead Capture & Actions:** Direct resume PDF download, interactive screenshot lightbox galleries with slide navigation, external live deployment links, social profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub), and direct inquiry submission via Web3Forms modal.
- **Constraints:** Must maintain rapid initial page load, smooth animations without performance degradation, responsive layout integrity down to 360px viewports, and zero fabrication of unconfirmed claims.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims:** Showcase working code, architectural responsibilities, genuine screenshots, and verifiable deployments rather than abstract self-praise.
2. **Speed to Substance:** Enable visitors to immediately understand Alvincent's core stack, specialty, and track record within the first 5 seconds.
3. **End-to-End Cohesion:** Present UI, system architecture, background jobs, and AI capabilities as unified product engineering rather than disconnected disciplines.
4. **Uncompromising Craft:** Keep every interaction, transition, typographic hierarchy, and responsive breakpoint crisp, intentional, and bug-free.
